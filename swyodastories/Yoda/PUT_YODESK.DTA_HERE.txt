Put your own legally-owned yodesk.dta here.

It sits next to Yodesk.exe on the original CD or in an installed copy.
If your copy is still SZDD-compressed (starts with "SZDD" instead of
"VERS" when you check the first 4 bytes), decompress it first - e.g.
Windows' own EXPAND.EXE reads that format natively:

    expand YODESK.DT_ yodesk.dta

Delete this file once yodesk.dta is here; it's just a placeholder so
the folder isn't empty in the zip.
